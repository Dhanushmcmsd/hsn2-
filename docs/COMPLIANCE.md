# Compliance

## GST Compliance Approach
- GST rates sourced from CBIC/fallback data and synced on schedule.
- Change history tracked in `gst_rate_history` and change logs.

## Retention
- Audit trail retained for 7 years to support tax and compliance needs.

## Export for Audit
- Export via `/admin/audit-log/export` (CSV).
- GST report exports available in CSV/PDF endpoints.

## Disclaimer
For official GST filing, verify with GSTIN portal.
